Turtleman Comics

Turtleman is a freecomic series, published as printable A4 PDF issues. The series covers AI, business, travel, language, technology and modern institutional nonsense.

Old-school superhero on yellowing paper, generated with just enough AI slop to be honest about it. To be clear, we reject slop-shamers and all that they represent. Download the full issues as PDFs and read them wherever you like.
